# AppLord

A DrugLord-style command-line trading game. You have 30 days to get one app into production at Consolidated Mutual Trust & Fidelity, a fictional 100,000-person bank that was last modernized in 1987.

Trade enterprise contraband (Jira tickets, Visio diagrams, Excel macros, firewall exceptions, test evidence, exec signatures, prod access, Oracle licenses) between siloed departments. Pass six waterfall gates in order: REQ → ARB → SEC → UAT → CAB → PROD. Survive a 30-second requirements walkthrough (a tribute to Desert Bus: your focus drifts toward a nap, and you have to keep steering it back), play Bullsh*t Bingo against the live transcript of surprise all-hands calls, dodge Internal Audit, survive reorgs, and pay back Brenda from the PMO at 5% a day.

Each run starts by picking one of five characters, original creations loosely in the spirit of Office Space, The Office and Silicon Valley:

- **Mitch Scanlon** (Product) gets 25% more gate funding and a head start on Jira, but opens $8,000 in debt.
- **Anton Gilroy** (Technology) gets a 150k token quota and lighter SEC and CAB gates, but Internal Audit shows up twice as often.
- **Paige Holloway** (Design) needs half the Visio and Test Evidence and handles meetings better, but starts with $3,000 and an 80k quota.
- **Duane Kessler** (Delivery) starts with a RACI and can take CAB on any day, but loses sanity to a status report every Friday.
- **Phil Lundgren** (Executive) signs his own paperwork and has a $20,000 credit limit, but gets a 60k quota and nods off faster in the walkthrough.

Each character also has a team and one special move: Mitch's improv pitch, Anton's crypto mining, Paige's hand-drawn diagrams, Duane's one-time re-baseline, and Phil's weekend work. Payroll is due every Friday. Brenda covers a shortfall while you have credit; after that HR starts letting people go, and you can fire someone yourself for quick cash.

A run ends on the spot if your sanity hits zero, your team hits zero, or your PMO debt reaches twice Brenda's credit limit.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. It's a single self-contained file. Progress and the hall of fame are saved to your browser's local storage.

Desktop keys: `T` travel · `G` submit gate · `P` PMO · `C` CIO sign-off · `Q` give up · `?` playbook.

*A work of fiction. Any resemblance to your employer is your employer's problem.*
