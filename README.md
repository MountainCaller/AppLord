# AppLord

A DrugLord-style command-line trading game. You have 30 days to get one app into production at Consolidated Mutual Trust & Fidelity, a fictional 100,000-person bank that was last modernized in 1987.

Trade enterprise contraband (Jira tickets, Visio diagrams, Excel macros, firewall exceptions, test evidence, exec signatures, prod access, Oracle licenses) between siloed departments. Pass six waterfall gates in order: REQ → ARB → SEC → UAT → CAB → PROD. Survive a 30-second requirements walkthrough (a tribute to Desert Bus: your focus drifts toward a nap, and you have to keep steering it back), play Bullsh*t Bingo against the live transcript of surprise all-hands calls, dodge Internal Audit, survive reorgs, and pay back Brenda from the PMO at 5% a day.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. It's a single self-contained file. Progress and the hall of fame are saved to your browser's local storage.

Desktop keys: `T` travel · `G` submit gate · `P` PMO · `C` CIO sign-off · `Q` give up · `?` playbook.

*A work of fiction. Any resemblance to your employer is your employer's problem.*
